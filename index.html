A football club for Middleton and Overton

Middleton & Overton Sports FC is an adult grassroots football club based at Middleton Park in Middleton, Morecambe. We represent the villages of Middleton and Overton and welcome players and supporters from Heysham and the wider Lancashire coast.

The first team competes in the Mid Lancashire Football League First Division. Live standings, upcoming fixtures and recent results are published below through The FA Full-Time, and the About page explains who we are today. Club history covers the Overton, Middleton and TIC Dynamos lineage. Local businesses can sponsor the club or get in touch.

Our sponsors

Local and national partners help keep grassroots football going at Middleton Park. We are grateful to our sponsors for backing Middleton & Overton Sports FC.

League table, fixtures & results

Middleton & Overton Sports FC play in the First Division of the Mid Lancashire Football League, an English Football Accredited competition for adult grassroots sides across Lancashire. Home matches are played at Middleton Park.

Club questions

Short answers about where we play, the league we compete in, and how to get involved with Middleton & Overton Sports FC.

Where does Middleton & Overton Sports FC play?

Home matches are at Middleton Park, Middleton Rd, Middleton, Morecambe, LA3 3LG. There is a map and directions on this page under Contact.

What league does Middleton & Overton Sports FC play in?

The first team competes in the First Division of the Mid Lancashire Football League. Live standings, fixtures and results are on our league section and on The FA Full-Time.

Where is Middleton Park?

Middleton Park is in Middleton, on the south side of Morecambe, with Overton and Heysham nearby. The postcode is LA3 3LG. Open the ground in Google Maps.

How can I contact Middleton & Overton Sports FC?

Use the contact form on this page, or message the club on Facebook or Instagram.

How can I sponsor Middleton & Overton Sports FC?

Pitchside boards, matchday, online and player sponsorship, plus donations, are listed on our sponsor page. You can also contact the club to talk through a package.

How can I get involved with the club?

We are a community grassroots club. If you would like to play, help, or support adult football in Middleton and Morecambe, get in touch or read more about the club.

When does Middleton & Overton Sports FC play?

Kick-off times and opponents change through the season. Use the fixtures widget or the official FA Full-Time division page for the next match. We do not publish a separate fixture list on this site.

Contact Middleton & Overton Sports FC

Get in touch about playing, supporting or sponsoring the club. You can send a message below, or reach us on Facebook and Instagram. We are based at Middleton Park in Middleton, Morecambe.
