Middleton & Overton Sports FC history

Overview

The club lineage has moved from Overton-based sides through shared Middleton & Overton setups, the TIC Dynamos era, to today’s Middleton & Overton Sports FC, a grassroots football club based at Middleton Park, Morecambe. About the club covers who we are now; this page keeps the historical record as it was written down.

Name evolution

Overton FC → Overton + Middleton FC → TIC Dynamos of O+M → TIC Dynamos of M+O → Middleton & Overton Sports FC

Re-started

2006/07 — B Doyle & K Riley
